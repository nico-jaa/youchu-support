## Help

### Everyday organizing

#### Start with one thing

When you first open Youchu, add an item straight away or choose and rename a few useful locations first. Setting up locations is optional. Later, tap + in Items and choose Manual entry, for example to record a spare key.

Tap Storage location to choose a room, cupboard or drawer. If the right place isn't there, tap New location at the top of the list or below an empty search result. Enter its name and parent, then tap Create and Use. Your item entries stay in place, and you still need to save the item separately. Locations you explicitly create remain even if you cancel item entry. You can also leave the location unassigned for now.

Add an expiry date when the item has one; keys don't need a date. Check the details and save. It will appear in your catalog. You can add a photo or notes later.

Without a photo, Youchu shows a category illustration matched to the name, such as dairy, fruit or cleaning supplies. Unknown names get a general storage image. It's an illustration, not an identification result. Your own photo takes priority; removing it brings the illustration back.

Bought the same thing again? Tap Add another in its details to reuse the name, unit, location and aliases. Check the new packaging date. Reusing the old photo is optional. Manually added items never merge just because their names match.

For several things at once, open More entry options and turn on Keep adding after saving. A successful save opens a blank draft with just the location kept. Capture never starts automatically. Photo or voice suggestions can fill empty fields in the current draft; replacing existing content or choosing a date still needs your review.

#### Find it next time

Search an item name in Items, such as “keys”, to see where it lives. Can't remember the name? Search its location or browse the Locations tab.

Build up to three levels, such as Kitchen › Fridge › Chilled shelf. Start with the places you use most; you don't need to organize the whole home at once. Check full paths when cupboards share a name.

Moved something? Open its details, tap Edit item, choose the new location and save. Next time, you'll see its new home.

Call a power adapter a “charger”? Expand More details · aliases when editing to add the name you use. Search keeps the main name and shows when an alias is the reason for a match.

Often check opened items expiring soon in the fridge? Combine location, expiry, opening status and a keyword, then name and save the filter. The first three appear below search. Date conditions update daily; temporary changes never overwrite a saved filter. If a location is deleted, edit the saved filter to choose another.

Moved several things? Tap Select in the directory, choose from the current results, then Move selected. Confirm the full destination path. Quantities and dates stay the same, and you can undo right afterwards.

#### Add an address for another home

For more than one home or storage site, open a location and choose Edit location from the more menu. Type a city, street or unit in Street address. You can also add it while creating a location. It's optional; your rooms, cupboards and drawers work as usual.

Standing there now? Tap Use current location, read the explanation and continue with the system's location permission. Check the result, then tap Use this address. Add a unit number if needed and save the location. Positioning can suggest a nearby address, not the cupboard your item is in.

A child location without its own address inherits the nearest parent's address. Item details show that address. Clear and save a separate address to remove it; if a parent still has one, inheritance resumes.

If permission is declined, only approximate location is available or you're offline, type the address or leave it blank. Youchu does not track in the background or store coordinates. Address lookup uses Apple geocoding without sending household records.

#### Same names, quantities and Add another

One stock record shares a location, unit, packaging expiry date and opening state. Directory counts refer to records; they do not add unlike units such as bottles and cups into one stock total.

Items with the same name may have different batches or dates. Use Add another to make a new record and check its date. Manual entry does not merge items just because names match; linked stock can be viewed in the details.

Before using, discarding, opening or packing some stock, check the quantity and original unit. Partial opening or moving creates linked records while retaining the remainder; a bottle count is not a volume in millilitres.

#### Record opening part of your stock

Tap Record opening in the item details and choose the quantity and date. Enter after-opening days only when the packaging provides them; otherwise leave them blank. This does not mark the stock as used up.

Opening part splits the stock into linked opened and unopened records without changing the total quantity. The packaging expiry date remains. When an after-opening duration exists, expiry lists and reminders use the earlier of that date and the packaging expiry date.

Dates use calendar days and do not assess safety by the hour. Check packaging instructions, storage conditions and the product itself. Recognition suggestions and reminders are not food or medicine safety conclusions.

#### Remember an expiry date

When adding or editing an item, tap Expiry date and choose the year, month and day on its packaging. If it's unreadable or there is no date, leave it blank.

The Expiry tab separates expired items, items expiring today and those expiring in the next 1–7 days. Items without dates also have their own filter.

For a heads-up, enable Settings → Expiry reminders. The list always uses a 7-day window; changing notification lead time doesn't change that window.

#### Finished it, or thrown it away?

Finished one bottle or thrown away one cup? Tap Use some in the details, choose used or discarded, check the quantity and confirm. All selects the remaining quantity without submitting. Swiping left on a list record opens the same review.

If some remain, the record stays active with its location, dates and opening details unchanged. History records the action. Only zero remaining moves it to Processed and stops reminders. Tap Undo straight away. Restoring a processed record later brings back the quantity from the last full processing action, keeping its original dates; it may already be expired.

When you no longer need a record, choose Delete item from its details menu. This permanently removes the record and its own stock history; photo copies are cleaned up only when no other item uses them. Photos originals are unaffected. Moving history may still retain the name, quantity and location recorded at the time; see Moving drafts, interruptions and history. Deleting a storage location lets you choose another home or leave items unassigned; it does not delete items.

Before starting over, check what you need to keep. Settings → Local data → Clear local data permanently removes active and processed items, photos, locations and addresses, stock history, saved filters, all moves, containers, moving history and operation drafts, and resets reminders. This cannot be undone. System permissions and independent preferences such as appearance are not reset; manage historical device backups separately. Do not clear data to troubleshoot saving.

#### Keep a shortcut on your Home Screen

Touch and hold Youchu's icon for photo, voice or manual entry, or to find an item. Capture never starts automatically. If you have unsaved work, the shortcut waits until the current page closes.

Add a Youchu widget to your Home Screen: Expiry overview covers today and the next 7 days; Favorite location lets you pick a location; Quick entry opens photo, voice or manual entry. You can also add an expiry count or camera shortcut to the lock screen.

Expiry overview shows counts by default. Touch and hold to edit the widget and enable details. iOS may delay updates. If a count looks stale, open Youchu or retry refreshing in Settings. Used, discarded or permanently deleted items leave the next snapshot.

### Photos & voice

#### Take a photo, then check it

Tap Photo entry in Items to take a picture or choose one from Photos. Keep the name and date clear; try a different angle for shiny packaging.

Youchu looks for names and dates on the device, then opens the editable draft. A single name suggestion can fill a blank name; existing input stays unchanged. Open Suggestions and original text to check whether dates mean production, expiry or best before, then select one. A calculated date is offered only with an explicit production date and shelf life; check the packaging.

Choose the location and check the name and dates in the draft, then tap Confirm and save. Missing details can be typed with Fill in manually. Leave uncertain dates blank. Retaking a photo or retrying recognition preserves existing entries. Taking a photo never adds an item automatically.

Can't take a photo? Choose one from your library or fill in manually. To restore access, allow Youchu's camera in iPhone Settings. Replacing a photo or trying recognition again won't overwrite fields you've already selected; check them once more before saving.

#### Say it, then review it

Voice entry currently supports on-device Simplified Chinese. Open Voice entry, then tap Start speaking when ready. For example, say in Chinese: “燕麦奶放在厨房冰箱，明天到期。” Describe one item at a time, using location names you've already created. Changing the interface to English does not change this recognition language; manual forms accept names and notes in other languages.

Tap Stop and finish transcript to open your editable draft. A clear name, one matching location and an unambiguous expiry date fill only blank fields; existing edits stay unchanged. Check the draft and tap Confirm and save. Use Edit transcript to correct misheard words, or open Suggestions and original text to check the evidence or choose alternatives. Recording never adds an item automatically.

Relative Chinese dates such as tomorrow or in three days use the date when input began. Find that reference date in Suggestions and original text. Include the year for calendar dates. Multiple dates, duplicate location names and uncertain expressions need your confirmation; they won't be guessed.

Voice stays on the device; no audio file is saved. If permissions, resources or the device don't support recognition, type Chinese in the text box or use the manual form in any language. Leaving the page, backgrounding or an audio interruption stops recording without automatic restart.

#### When photo or voice entry won't work?

First check whether it's a permission issue, unsupported device or inaccurate recognition. Use Open permission settings in Help, or find Youchu in iOS Settings to check camera, microphone and speech access. Paths may vary by iOS version. Opening Help doesn't request permissions.

Voice requires on-device Simplified Chinese support and available resources. If unsupported or temporarily unavailable, try later or use manual entry. Youchu won't switch to cloud recognition. Each recording lasts at most 45 seconds; restart it yourself after backgrounding, leaving or an interruption.

If text isn't recognized in a photo, try a well-lit, close picture of the packaging and dates, or choose another image. Type blurred, incomplete or uncertain dates from the packaging; don't guess shelf life from appearance. The system picker shares only photos you select, without full-library permission.

Keep and review your draft before recording again or leaving. Unavailable capture or permissions don't block manual entry or existing local items. Recognition results become records only after you explicitly save.

### Expiry reminders

#### Let Youchu remind you

Open Settings → Expiry reminders, enable reminders and allow system notifications. The default is 7 days ahead at 9 a.m. local time. Choose 1 or 3 days or another time, then tap Save reminder rule.

Missing a notification? Check the scheduled counts and coverage date first. If there is an error, tap Check reminders again. In Settings → Notifications → Youchu, check banners and sounds, along with Focus and Scheduled Summary.

Items due at the same time share one notification. Each item gets one reminder for the same expiry date. If reminders are waiting, opening the app again schedules the nearest dates first. You can check the expiry list anytime.

Expired items and items with no future reminder time before expiry don't get catch-up notifications. Declining notifications still lets you manage items and view expiry dates.

Want an earlier reminder for just this record? Open Item reminders in its details. Follow default, choose the expiry day or 1/3/7 days before, or turn this record off. Rules are kept while the master switch is off or permission is unavailable. A record without a date becomes eligible once you add one. Saving a rule does not request permission.

When you need more time, save rule changes and choose Remind me later for tonight, tomorrow morning or a future time. Only one later reminder is kept per record; the expiry date stays. Stock processing, deletion and date changes reconcile requests. If you see Rule saved. Reminder not scheduled., your data is safe to keep while you retry scheduling.

### Packing for a move

#### Start a move: pack, add stock and use the next box

Open Moving in the tab bar to start a move; the first box gets a number automatically. In List, select items and check quantities before confirming. In Cards, choose an item and a destination, then use the button or drag the open card into a destination. A single unit moves on release; a record with more than one unit opens a quantity check first.

Container information can include purpose/notes, a preferred source, categories matched by names/aliases or keywords. Priorities change ordering while leaving other items available; combined filters only narrow candidates and do not edit items.

Add an unregistered item directly to the current box, review it and save. Choose the next box when ready. Numbers keep increasing; removing an empty box or undoing does not reassign numbers already used.

Containers shows thumbnails, names and quantities. Viewing another container keeps the current packing target. Explicitly switching restores that container’s own packing draft.

Switch between List and Cards without submitting your list selection. The hand control swaps the two workspace columns and remembers your choice. Scroll, tap a preview or use the heading arrows to browse; hovering never switches the target or moves stock.

#### Find boxes, move a container and put items away

Directory search also finds packed stock and shows its container number and current storage location. A container location is a storage place you maintain, not live delivery or GPS tracking.

When a whole container moves, update its current location in Containers. This changes where the container is; it does not unpack its contents.

Before unpacking, explicitly choose Put away in, then put away one item, a selection or all remaining contents. You can put away part in the original unit; the remainder stays in the container. To transfer stock, explicitly select the destination container.

Before submitting, check the container number, full destination path and quantity. If records changed, review their current state and your selection before retrying. Do not delete data to resolve a conflict.

#### Moving drafts, interruptions and history

Moving selections, quantities, filters, container search terms, the current container and put-away destination are saved automatically on this device so you can continue later. These are operation drafts; only confirmed packing or putting away moves stock. Reopening checks deleted or changed records.

Let the draft save before leaving. If you see a draft-save error, stay on the page and retry. Force-quitting, a storage failure or clearing data may lose the latest draft. Unsaved photo, voice and ordinary item forms follow their own page warnings.

Undo reverses the latest complete operation while the related records remain unchanged. The result and Undo share the status row above the action button. If another change causes a conflict, check the current location and quantities before correcting them manually.

Switch tabs to leave without finishing the move; return to Moving to continue. Finish and archive becomes available once all active packed stock is put away. Containers and history remain in Past moves. Removing an empty container or deleting one item does not erase independent moving history. Clear local data also deletes every other item and location, so check the confirmation carefully.

### Data & support

#### Before you change phones

Items, locations, photos, saved filters, moves, containers, history and moving drafts currently stay on this iPhone. There is no Youchu account-based cloud sync, so installing on another device does not automatically bring them across. The developer has no remote catalog copy to restore.

Before changing phones or deleting the app, check that your device backup has completed. You can use iOS backup and migration tools. What is actually restored depends on the system backup and restore result.

In-app export and import are not currently available. If saving fails, keep the form open and contact support. Don't delete the app or clear its data as a troubleshooting step.

#### Filled it in, but couldn't save?

Keep the form open. Your entries remain when saving fails. Read the message, check the name and chosen location and make sure your iPhone has enough storage, then try again.

If the item changed elsewhere, copy or note your current entries before closing and reopening it. Check the latest record before editing so you don't overwrite an existing change.

The form closes after a successful save. If it still fails, use Contact support below to describe the steps. You don't need to include item photos or private notes.

#### Change language and reading settings

Youchu supports Simplified Chinese and English and follows the iOS app language setting. Find Youchu in iPhone Settings and look for Language; availability depends on the system version and languages you have added.

Changing the interface language does not translate your item names, locations, notes or aliases, or change voice recognition beyond on-device Simplified Chinese. Dates follow your region for display and use Gregorian year, month and day for storage and comparison.

Use system text-size and accessibility settings for larger text. Help and privacy paragraphs can be selected and copied. Both remain available offline in the app when the website cannot be reached.

#### Can't open the website?

These answers work offline. The privacy policy is also in Youchu Settings, so you don't have to wait for a website.

Website links try GitHub Pages first, falling back to GitCode on failure or after about 4 seconds. If your browser still won't open it, expand GitCode backup website in Help and open it directly. The public website may be in Simplified Chinese; this in-app English help is available offline. GitCode may also show rate limits or ask for its own sign-in. You can keep reading offline in the app or use the email address below; no Youchu account is needed.

Still stuck? Email me using the address below. Tell me where you got stuck and what happened. Contact support can help draft the text for you to check, copy and send yourself.

#### View, delete or ask about information

Manage items, locations and photos on this device by editing or permanently deleting an item in its details. Marking used or discarded keeps the record. Deleting one item may leave independent moving history and photos still reused by other items. To erase all moving history and operation drafts, confirm Settings → Local data → Clear local data; this also deletes other items, locations, photos and saved filters and resets reminders. It cannot be undone. Check what you need to keep first.

The developer has no remote copy of the catalog and cannot restore or delete local records for you. Photos originals, device backups, browser data and sent emails are managed in their own services. Don't delete the app or clear data to troubleshoot.

To ask about support email information received by the developer, email bearrabbit1002@outlook.com with the subject “Youchu privacy request”. Explain what you'd like to access, correct, delete or withdraw. Use the original contact address when possible. Don't attach your catalog, photos, recordings or identity documents.

We verify requests and reply within applicable legal deadlines. If some information must be kept by law, we explain why and the available options. The Privacy policy explains information scope, providers, retention and withdrawing permissions.
